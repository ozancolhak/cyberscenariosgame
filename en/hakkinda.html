About the Project

"Cyber Security Simulations" is an educational platform where real-life cyber crises are enacted through interactive decision-making scenarios. For Cyber Professionals: Users take strategic decisions against various scenarios in 6 different roles and experience cybersecurity processes. For Companies and Institutions: It consists of interactive crisis simulations aimed at increasing employees' awareness of information and cyber security. This project has been developed to increase cyber awareness in educational institutions, companies, and for anyone interested in cybersecurity.

Use Cases

Schools, universities, companies, and cybersecurity enthusiasts can use these simulations for personnel training, awareness-raising programs, and crisis management practices.

Technologies Used

Developed as a simple and high-performance web application based on HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It offers an interactive experience focused on gamification techniques and decision-making.
