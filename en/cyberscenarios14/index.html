Virtualization Network Issue Simulation

Welcome! Your company's critical e-commerce platform is hosted on your virtualization infrastructure and is experiencing serious network problems. Customer complaints are increasing, latency and packet loss are at abnormal levels. As a Senior Network and Virtualization Engineer, you are tasked with finding the source of this complex problem and ensuring system stability.

Simulation Role

  • Your Role: Senior Network and Virtualization Engineer As the senior engineer responsible for the network performance of the virtualization infrastructure, you must find the source of the problem and solve it permanently.
  • Virtualization Platform Your entire infrastructure including VMware ESXi, vCenter, virtual switches (vSwitch), and port groups.
  • Physical Network Infrastructure Physical switches, cabling, and routers that servers are connected to.

Key Metrics

  • Service Quality (QoS) Latency and packet loss rate affecting customer experience. (Initial: Critical)
  • Resource Usage Effective utilization rate of physical host resources like network, CPU, RAM. (Initial: Abnormal)
  • Customer Reputation Damage to company reliability. (Initial: Low)
  • Cost Financial equivalent of service interruptions and potential customer loss. (Initial: Increasing)
