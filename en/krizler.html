Choose Your Role

Choose a specialization in your cybersecurity career journey. Each role will lead you to special crisis scenarios that will test your diverse skills. Determine your strategy and intervene.

SOC Analyst

The first line of defense. Monitor alerts, detect anomalies, and be the first to find the real threat signal within the noise.

Incident Responder

The cool-headed professional of the crisis moment. Stop active attacks, collect evidence, and reclaim the system from the enemy.

Penetration Tester

An expert who tests systems through the eyes of the enemy, finds vulnerabilities, and proves them within ethical boundaries.

Cybersecurity Engineer

Guardian of systems and technologies. Deeply analyze vulnerabilities, harden complex systems, and defend proactively.

Network Security Engineer

The heart of network infrastructure. Create secure network designs, monitor traffic flow, and establish defense mechanisms.

Cybersecurity Manager

Strategist and leader. Translate technical crises into business language, manage risk, plan the budget, and lead the board.
