Breach of Trust Simulation

Welcome, Incident Responder! Today you will face a crisis that goes beyond technology and envelops your company: **The Insider Threat**. Leaked data, plummeting reputation, and shaken trust... In this simulation, you will not only solve technical problems but also make difficult decisions full of human psychology, company culture, and ethical dilemmas. Every choice you make will determine the course of the crisis and the fate of the company. Are you ready?

Simulation Roles

  • Your Role: Incident Responder - You are responsible for leading the investigation, making critical decisions, and managing the company's response to the crisis. Your choices will directly affect the outcome.
  • CEO Ms. Hande - The company's top executive, focused on general business continuity and public perception.
  • CFO Mr. Arda - Responsible for the company's financial health, closely monitoring losses and potential impacts on future investments.
  • Legal Counsel Ms. Zeynep - Oversees legal compliance and advises on public statements to mitigate legal risks.
  • Sales Manager Ms. Ayse Sari - Your teammate who first discovered the leak.
  • Potential Suspects (Elif, Can, Burak, Ayse Sari) - Employees who may be involved, either as perpetrators or victims.
