Identity and Access Crisis

Welcome to ConnectSphere, Cyber Security Engineer! After integrating the customer portal with SSO (Single Sign-On), alarm bells started ringing. The help desk is flooded with complaints about users having unexpected privileges and some accounts being compromised. Your mission: Find the root cause of this complex identity crisis, close security gaps, and strengthen system architecture.
