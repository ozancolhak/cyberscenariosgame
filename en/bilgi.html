Information Security Simulations

Identify security risks in your daily workflow with these simulations designed for employees and strengthen your company's cyber defense by reacting correctly. Each scenario is prepared according to ISO 27001 standards.

Medium

Misdirected Email
(ISO 27001: A.13)

You accidentally sent an important and confidential email to an irrelevant recipient. How do you manage this situation and prevent the error from spreading? Test your communication security rules.

High

Unauthorized Access Request
(ISO 27001: A.9)

An access request for a critical system came from an unauthorized employee. How do you evaluate this request and apply security protocols? Test your access control knowledge.
Easy

Confidential Info on Open Desk
(ISO 27001: A.11.2.9)

You left your desk with confidential documents and an unlocked computer screen. Identify the potential risks of this situation and learn how to apply the 'Clean Desk' policy.
